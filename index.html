Privacy Policy

Kabadiwala Dai is a doorstep scrap-collection marketplace connecting customers with pickup partners across Nepal. This page explains, in plain terms, what information we collect through the app, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Effective date: August 16, 2026 Last updated: August 16, 2026 Applies to: the Kabadiwala Dai Android app (Customer & Partner)

01Information we collect

We only collect what the app's actual features need to work — nothing is collected for advertising, and we don't run any third-party analytics or ad SDKs.

Category What, specifically Why
Account info Full name, email, phone number, password (stored hashed) Creating and securing your account, letting partners and customers identify each other for a pickup
Location Pickup address, and precise GPS coordinates when you use "Use Current Location" or the map picker Matching your pickup request to the nearest available partner, and showing partners exactly where to go
Photos Photos you optionally attach to a pickup request Helping the assigned partner recognize what's being collected before they arrive
Transaction & payment info Order history, scrap categories and weights, payout amounts, and which payment method you choose (cash, eSewa, or Khalti) Calculating payouts, keeping a record you can review, and processing payment
Partner verification For partner accounts only: business details, vehicle info, and identity documents submitted during signup Verifying partners before they're allowed to accept pickup jobs

We do not collect precise location in the background — only when the app is open and you take an action that needs it.

02How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account, and let you sign in securely.
  • To connect a customer's pickup request with the nearest available, verified partner.
  • To let a partner navigate to a pickup, weigh the scrap collected, and record what was paid out.
  • To show you your own order and transaction history within the app.
  • To respond if you contact us for support.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse of the platform.

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

03Who we share it with

We share only what's necessary to actually complete a pickup or a payment — never for marketing purposes.

Your assigned pickup partner Payment processors (eSewa, Khalti) Our hosting provider Law enforcement, if legally required

Pickup partners

When a pickup is assigned, the partner sees your name, address, phone number, and pickup details — enough to complete the job. Customers see the assigned partner's name and business details.

Payment processors

If you pay via eSewa or Khalti, the transaction is handled directly by that provider under their own privacy policy — we don't store your card, wallet, or banking credentials ourselves.

Service providers

Our backend and database are hosted by third-party infrastructure providers who process data only on our instructions, to keep the app running.

We never sell personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

04How long we keep it

We keep account and transaction data for as long as your account is active, so your order history stays available to you. If you deactivate your account, we retain transaction records only as long as reasonably necessary for legal, tax, and dispute-resolution purposes, then delete or anonymize them.

05Your choices

  • Review and edit your profile information any time from the app's Profile tab.
  • Deactivate your account directly in the app — Profile → Deactivate Account. This signs you out and stops your account from being used for new pickups.
  • Location access can be turned off in your device's system settings; the app will still work, but you'll need to type your pickup address manually instead of using "Use Current Location."
  • Contact us (see below) to request a copy of your data, or full deletion beyond what deactivation covers.

06Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections between the app and our servers and hashed password storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data protected and to respond quickly if anything looks wrong.

07Children's privacy

Kabadiwala Dai is intended for users aged 18 and older, since booking a pickup and receiving payment both require the legal capacity to transact. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

08Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we handle your data, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, let you know inside the app. Continued use of Kabadiwala Dai after a change means you accept the updated policy.
