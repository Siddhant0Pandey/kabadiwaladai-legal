Delete Your Account

How to close your Kabadiwala Dai account and have your personal data removed — whether or not you still have the app installed.

Applies to: Kabadiwala Dai Customer & Partner accounts Last updated: August 16, 2026

How to request deletion

  1. 1 Deactivate in the app (immediate)

    Open Kabadiwala Dai → Profile → Deactivate Account. This signs you out right away and stops the account from being usable for any new pickups or jobs.

  2. 2 Request full data deletion

    Email pandeysiddhant0922@gmail.com from the address on your account (or include your registered phone number), with the subject "Delete my account." Do this even if you've already uninstalled the app.

  3. 3 We confirm and delete

    We verify the request is really you, then permanently delete your data as described below — within 30 days of your request.

What gets deleted, and what we keep

Data Outcome
Name, email, phone, password Deleted
Saved addresses & pickup photos Deleted
Partner verification documents Deleted
Completed order & payment records Retained, with personal identifiers removed where possible, for as long as required by applicable tax and financial record-keeping law

Why some records are kept: transaction history tied to completed payments is retained briefly for legal, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes, the same way a bank or payment provider would. It's disconnected from your name/contact info wherever possible and is never used for anything beyond that requirement.

Questions about your data
